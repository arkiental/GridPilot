GRIDPILOT / DESIGN EXPLORATION

Five ways to see your desktop.

A display thumbnail becomes the placement surface.

Design brief ↗

Shared interaction: capture the selected display before the panel opens, retain a low-resolution thumbnail, and draw a translucent grid over it. Keep the thumbnail frozen while dragging; apply the window placement on release. The thumbnails in these mockups are illustrative. These concepts do not change the running application.
